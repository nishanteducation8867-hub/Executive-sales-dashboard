Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Sales Performance and Profitability Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and enables users to explore sales performance across time, countries, sales channels, product categories, and regions.

The report is designed to help business stakeholders monitor revenue, gross profit, order volume, quantity sold, and gross margin through KPI cards, interactive slicers, and data visualizations.
🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor key sales and profitability KPIs in one executive-level view.
- Compare monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Analyze revenue performance across countries.
- Understand revenue contribution by sales channel.
- Evaluate gross profit across product categories and regions.
- Enable interactive exploration using report slicers.
- Present business data in a clear and accessible visual format.
🧰 Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query – data preparation and transformation (where applicable in the report workflow)
- DAX Measures – KPI calculations and business metrics
- Data Modeling – report model using sales and calendar tables
- Data Visualization – KPI cards, line chart, bar charts, pie chart, doughnut chart, and treemap
📂 Power BI Report Structure
The report model and visuals use the following tables:
Table	Role in the Report
SalesDataTable	Sales transaction fields used for country, sales channel, product, product category, and region analysis
Calendar Table	Date-related fields, including month and quarter, used for time-based analysis and filtering
All_Measures	Central location for the report's DAX measures


📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard displays five key measures:
KPI	Description
Total Quantity	Total quantity of products sold
Total Revenue	Total sales revenue generated
Total Orders	Number of orders recorded
Total Gross Profit	Gross profit generated from sales
Gross Margin	Gross profit expressed as a percentage of revenue


These KPIs provide a high-level snapshot of sales volume, revenue generation, and profitability.
📈 Dashboard Visualizations
The report includes the following visual analyses:
Visualization	Analysis
Line Chart	Monthly comparison of Total Revenue and Total Gross Profit
Clustered Bar Charts	Country-wise revenue comparison
Pie Chart	Revenue contribution by Sales Channel
Doughnut Chart	Gross profit contribution by Product Category
Treemap	Regional gross profit comparison
KPI Cards	Total Quantity, Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Gross Profit, and Gross Margin


🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)
Users can interact with the report using four slicers:
- Quarter – filter the report by quarter.
- Month Name – focus on a selected month.
- Product Category – analyze a specific product category.
- Product – explore performance for an individual product.
The slicers allow users to narrow the report view and compare performance across selected business dimensions.
💡 Business Questions This Dashboard Helps Explore
- How do revenue and gross profit change from month to month?
- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- How is revenue distributed across sales channels?
- Which product categories contribute to gross profit?
- How does gross profit vary across regions?
- How do the headline KPIs change when different quarters, months, products, or product categories are selected?
